Frequently asked questions

Every answer here is either confirmed by the game creator’s description or an explicit, honest “we don’t know.”

All questions

How do I start a trade?

Confirmed Stand together with another player on a trading board — a trade starts when two players share the board. You cannot start one alone.

How do I accept or cancel a trade?

Confirmed Both players must jump on the check mark to accept. Jumping on the cross cancels the trade. The deal only completes when both sides have accepted.

What does the plus sign do?

Confirmed Jumping on the plus sign asks the other player to add more Jellycats to the trade. It is the polite way to say “sweeten the offer.”

How do I grow my collection?

Confirmed Open plush crates — that is how you add Jellycats to your collection. After that, trading with friends helps you shape the inventory you want. See the beginner guide for the full loop.

Are all Jellycats equally rare?

Confirmed No. The creator description states that some Jellycats are rarer than others.
