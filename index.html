The core gameplay loop

Three steps, straight from the verified creator description.

  1. Open plush crates

    Opening plush crates is a documented way to grow your collection.

  2. Grow and review your collection

    Review what you pull and remember that the creator confirms some Jellycats are rarer than others.

  3. Trade with friends

    Use the trading board to place Jellycats you want to trade and work toward the inventory you want. Learn the trading controls.

Three symbols run every trade

The whole trading board works with a check mark, a cross, and a plus sign — you jump on them to act.

✓

Check mark

Both players jump on it to accept the trade.

✕

Cross

Jump on it to cancel the trade.

+

Plus sign

Jump on it to ask the other player to add more Jellycats.

Quick answers

The questions every new collector asks first.

How do I start a trade?

Stand together with another player on a trading board. A trade starts only when two players share the board.

Are all Jellycats equally rare?

No — the creator description says some Jellycats are rarer than others. Our review found no reliable source for exact tiers or odds.
